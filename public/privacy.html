Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Who we are

CVDS Digital Ventures ("CVDS", "we") is based in Perth, Western Australia. We help international technology companies enter the Australian market.

What we collect

When you submit our contact form or book a call, we collect the details you provide: your name, work email, company, country, website, technology sector, request type and the information you choose to share about your project.

Why we collect it

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, assess whether CVDS is the right fit, and follow up on your Australian market-entry project. We do not sell your information and do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Confidentiality and disclosure

Enquiries are treated as confidential. We do not share your information with third parties, such as potential partners, without your prior agreement, unless required by law.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to operate this website and handle enquiries, including GitHub Pages (website hosting), Formspree (form delivery) and Calendly (meeting scheduling). These providers may store data outside Australia.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry information only as long as it is needed for the purposes above, and take reasonable steps to protect it from misuse, loss and unauthorised access.

Your choices

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information at any time by contacting cecile.pagneux@cvds-ventures.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.
