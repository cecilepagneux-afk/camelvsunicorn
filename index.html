Before we talk

A few practical questions.

Do we need an Australian entity to get started?

You can begin with market research before establishing locally. Whether you need an Australian entity depends on your activities; legal and tax advice should come from qualified advisers.

Can we start with a limited engagement?

Yes. Start with one commercial question and a defined set of deliverables. Further work is agreed separately.

What does CVDS handle?

Market assessment, commercial preparation and relevant business introductions within the agreed scope. Product advice, contracting, regulatory approval and delivery remain with your team or appointed specialists unless expressly agreed otherwise.

How are fees agreed?

Fees reflect the scope, depth and deliverables of the mission. You receive a written proposal before work begins.
